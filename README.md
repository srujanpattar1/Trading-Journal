# Swing Trading Journal

This is my personal swing trading journal, shared for transparency and learning—not as a buy or sell signal service.

## Simple glossary

- **Swing trade:** Holding a stock for several days or weeks to capture a price move.
- **Entry price:** The price at which I buy a stock.
- **Stop-loss:** A pre-decided exit price that limits loss if the trade fails.
- **Target:** The price where I plan to take profit.
- **Position size:** The number of shares bought after calculating acceptable risk.
- **Risk per share:** The gap between entry price and initial stop-loss.
- **R / R-multiple:** Profit or loss measured against the amount initially risked.
- **Reward-to-risk (R:R):** Expected upside compared with the loss risked; higher is better only if realistic.
- **Pullback:** A temporary price dip during a broader upward trend.
- **Trend:** The market’s overall direction; this journal focuses on long trades in uptrends.
- **Partial sale:** Selling part of a position to secure some profit while keeping the rest open.
- **Breakeven:** A trade outcome with roughly no profit or loss, excluding charges.
- **Win rate:** The percentage of closed trades that made money.
- **Rule break:** A trade that ignored a planned risk, checklist, or process rule.
- **CSV / JSON backup:** Downloadable files used to keep a copy of journal data or open it in a spreadsheet.

## Note

Past trades do not predict future results. This journal is for education and self-review, not financial advice.
